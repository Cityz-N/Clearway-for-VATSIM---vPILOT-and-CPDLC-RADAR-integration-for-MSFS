Saved

Rules applied
  • Four tones in two pairs, written AB-CD.
  • Standard tones: A B C D E F G H J K L M P Q R S (no I, N or O).
  • No tone may appear more than once in a code.
  • Within each pair, tones are in ascending order (AB valid, BA not).
  • Extended (SELCAL32) adds T U V W X Y Z and 1–9. Extended codes here always contain at least one new tone, so they never overlap the standard set.

Saved codes are kept for 24 hours in Clearway on this PC (shared by the phone, PC page and MSFS panel), up to 10 per callsign. For simulation only: real SELCAL codes are assigned by the registrar (Aviation Spectrum Resources Inc.).

Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this PC. Scan the code with the phone's camera, then add the page to your home screen: on iPhone, Safari Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android, Chrome ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
